BENCHMARK COMPOSITION: Russell 2000 Growth focuses on the growth segment of the US small-cap Russell 2000 universe, whereas the S&P 500 primarily represents US large-cap equities. Thus Russell 2000 Growth matches a small-cap growth investment mandate more closely by size and style. However, the actual WOFNX portfolio holdings and capitalization profile must be examined before claiming it is the best economic benchmark for this fund.

DATA COMPARABILITY: WOFNX adjusted NAVs can reflect distributions, while Yahoo Finance's ^RUO and ^GSPC index levels typically represent price returns without dividend reinvestment. Therefore the comparisons may not be like-for-like total returns. Disclose this limitation or replace both index series with total-return series where available.

RISK-FREE METHODOLOGY: The rates are fixed using observations at the start of each investment horizon, as directed by the assignment. Year 1 and 2 use FRED DTB1YR (12-month bill, discount yield converted approximately to an effective rate); the full period uses DGS2 (2-year Treasury yield, a proxy rather than the requested USD 24-month Euromarket rate). Annualized Sharpe uses the horizon-specific weekly equivalent rate.

OVERALL QUALITATIVE ASSESSMENT: Over the full two-year period, WOFNX returned 18.13%, compared with 25.81% for Russell 2000 Growth and 35.60% for S&P 500. It underperformed the Russell by 7.68 percentage points, but had lower annualized volatility (15.99% versus 21.10%). Its worst rolling four-week loss (11.95%) was similar to the Russell's (12.04%). The Sharpe ratio (0.340) was positive but its Information Ratio (-0.360) was negative. This suggests lower risk than the specified small-cap growth benchmark but weak benchmark-relative performance over the sample. The conclusion remains conditional on the price-return versus total-return comparability issue and on the suitability of the assigned benchmark. Review portfolio holdings, style, fees and investment process before a final investment judgment.
